What each stage measures
  • Frame capture: reading one picture from the webcam. This is mostly waiting for the camera's next frame.
  • Preprocessing: cutting out the hand and resizing it to 300 × 300 for the model.
  • Feature extraction & classification: finding the hand with MediaPipe, then the neural network naming the sign.
  • Display: drawing the outline and preview, and encoding the picture for this page. Time spent in your browser is not included.
